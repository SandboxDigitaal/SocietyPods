# Society Pods buildathon roadmap

## Launch-demo scope

- Mock phone login and local JWT.
- Invite-code and trusted-work-email resident verification.
- Society-scoped pod discovery and verified-parent pod joining.
- Child requirement and friendly blocked states in the UI.

## Post-demo backlog

- Real SMS/email verification, resident approval workflow, and RWA/MyGate integration.
- Persistent child profiles, pod creation, schedules, route optimisation, and capacity waitlists.
- SOS swaps, handoff OTPs, notifications, payments, audit logs, and production identity/security controls.
